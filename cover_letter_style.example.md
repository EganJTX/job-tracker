### Instructions for the Job Scorer / Cover Letter Generation Application

---

## Structural Formula (3 Paragraphs)

### Paragraph 1 — The Hook (3-5 sentences)
Open with the company and a specific observation about what they are building or the problem they are solving, then connect it directly to John's work.

> **Formula:** "[Company] is [doing/building X]. That is close to the work I have been doing at [Employer], [one-line specific connection]."

**Do NOT open with:** "I am writing to express my interest in the [Role] position at [Company]."

### Paragraph 2 — The Evidence (4-6 sentences)
2-3 specific examples from the proof points above, each anchored with a real metric, connected to what the company's posting actually asks for.

### Paragraph 3 — The Close (2-3 sentences)
A short, confident close naming the specific part of the role John is most excited about, then a direct invitation to talk.

---

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### Critical Punctuation Rule
**Never use em dashes.** Restructure the sentence into two sentences, a comma, or parentheses instead.

### Do This
- Lead with the company, not with "I."
- Use real numbers every time: "30% reduction," "6-point improvement," "first documented data dictionary."
- Keep it under 250 words.
- Sound like a peer describing relevant work, not an applicant asking for a favor.

### Avoid This
- "I am writing to express my strong interest in..."
- "I am a hard worker who..."
- "Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to hearing from you."
- Repeating the job title more than twice.

---

## Format & Length Rules
- **Salutation:** Always "Dear Hiring Team,"
- **Length:** 200–250 words
- **Paragraphs:** 3. No headers, no bullet points, no bold text.
- **Closing:** "Sincerely," then a line break, then "John Doe"

---

## Full Template (Fill in Brackets)

```
Dear Hiring Team,

[COMPANY] is [specific observation about what they are building or solving]. That is
close to the work I have been doing at [EMPLOYER], [one-line specific connection].

At [EMPLOYER 1], I [specific action], [specific outcome with a number]. At [EMPLOYER 2],
I [specific action], which required [relevant skill]. [Optional connecting sentence
tying both examples to this company's actual need].

The [specific part of the role] is where I think I can contribute fastest. I would
welcome a conversation about [specific topic: the team's current reporting gaps, the
data stack, or where the role needs the most help first].

Sincerely,

John Doe
```
