Lighthouse is the hub of a three-part job search system. This page walks through how to set everything up and use it day to day. The Job Scorer bot is required. The Career Coach bot is optional but recommended.
Step 1 — Required
Set up Lighthouse

You're already here. Lighthouse is your pipeline tracker, identity file generator, and the hub that everything else connects to. Before setting up anything else, complete your profile in the Config tab.

1
Open the Config tab. Fill in the three sections: Identity (who you are and what you're looking for), Scorer Requirements (your hard gates for scoring), and optionally Cover Letter style.
2
Hit Save on each section as you complete it. This writes your identity.md file to the Lighthouse folder automatically.
3
Use the Copy buttons at the bottom of Config to get your scorer instructions and identity file. You'll need these in the next step.
If you already have an identity.md file from a previous session or another tool, drop it into the Lighthouse app folder and refresh — the app will read it automatically and populate your profile.
Step 2 — Required
Set up your Job Scorer bot

The Job Scorer is an AI bot — a Claude Project, a ChatGPT custom GPT, a Gemini Gem, or any AI tool that lets you set persistent instructions and attach files. It does the actual scoring work. You set it up once and reuse it every day.

1
Create a new project or custom bot in your preferred AI tool.
2
In the instructions field, paste the scorer instructions from Lighthouse Config (the middle Copy button).
3
Attach your identity.md file to the project. This is where the bot learns who you are — career history, target roles, compensation expectations, resume variants.
4
If you have a cover letter style guide, attach cover_letter_style.md as well. Without it, the bot scores but omits cover letter drafts.
When you update your identity.md in Lighthouse (new resume variants, changed target roles, updated comp floor), replace the attached file in your scorer bot too.
Daily use
Score, track, repeat
1
Find roles from LinkedIn, job boards, recruiter outreach, or anywhere else. When something looks interesting, paste the job description into your Job Scorer bot.
2
The bot returns a score, verdict, resume variant recommendation, flags, and cover letter angles. Review the output. If you decide to apply, tell the bot: "I applied to this one."
3
For roles you're passing on, no action needed — they'll be marked accordingly in the export.
4
At the end of the day (or whenever you've scored a batch), tell the bot: "Export today's scored roles as JSON." Save the file it produces.
5
Come back to Lighthouse. Hit Import in the top right. Select the JSON file. Lighthouse merges it into your pipeline — new roles are added, existing ones are updated without overwriting any status changes you've made here.
6
As roles progress — recruiter calls, interviews, offers — update their status directly in the Pipeline tab by expanding the role card. Use Save Changes to commit.
The pipeline is your record of truth. The scorer bot is ephemeral — it scores and exports. Lighthouse holds the history.
Optional companion
Career Coach bot
A separate AI bot that acts as your career sounding board — thought partner, interview prep, search accountability.

Set it up the same way as the Job Scorer bot, with different instructions and files:

1
Create a new project in your preferred AI tool. In the instructions field, paste the career coach instructions using the Copy button above.
2
Attach identity.md — the same file used by the Job Scorer. This is the foundation of who you are.
3
Attach your pipeline export from Lighthouse (Export button, top right). This gives the coach visibility into what you've applied to, where things stand, and what's been rejected or ghosted.
4
Maintain a network.md file — a running log of recruiter outreach, former colleague conversations, warm leads, and relationship status. Attach it to this project. This file lives in the coach project and is maintained there — it is not part of Lighthouse.

Identity
Produces identity.md — attach to all three projects
Who You Are ▾
The basics that anchor your identity across all three projects.
Your level, what you want more of, what you want less of, how you work best. e.g. "People leader who thrives building teams from scratch at growth-stage companies."
What you'll trade and what you won't. e.g. "Fully remote required." · "No travel above 20%." · "Will trade comp for strong culture."
Career Summary ▾
Paste your resume here. The scorer extracts proof points — team sizes, metrics, scope, wins — to reference when evaluating roles and writing cover letter angles. The better your resume leads with outcomes, the sharper the scoring.
Plain text is fine. Copy from Word, Google Doc, or PDF. Formatting doesn't matter — content does.
Target Roles ▾
Define what good looks like — role scope, company type, and domain. Be specific about what you want and what you don't.
Comma-separated — e.g. "Senior Manager of Analytics, Director of Data Science, Head of Business Intelligence"
e.g. "Early-stage startup, Series B–D" · "Software or SaaS — not a services or consulting firm"
e.g. "Open to all domains." · "Prefer FinTech or Healthcare but not exclusive." · "Avoiding retail and logistics."
Compensation ▾
The scorer uses this to assess whether a role credibly pays what you need given company size, stage, and industry norms.
The number below which you won't go — e.g. $100,000 · $150,000 · $200,000
e.g. "Floor is hard, no exceptions." · "Floor is fungible by $10–15K for strong culture fit." · "Targeting total comp, not just base."
Resume Variants optional ▾
Only fill this in if you maintain multiple resume versions. Leave blank and the scorer skips variant recommendations entirely. Example: Name: "FinTech Resume" · Triggers: "payments, banking, credit, financial services."
Extended Context ▾
Freeform narrative that grows over time. This is where your job search project will append new learnings — decision-making frameworks, interview notes, situational context, emerging patterns. Paste existing narrative here or leave blank to start. Claude amends this section, never the structured fields above.
Paste any existing identity narrative here. Your Career Transition project will append new content to this section over time.
